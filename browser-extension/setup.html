Your groceries. Your browser.

Set up once, send your basket when you’re ready.

Three steps to your first checkout

  1. Choose the stores you use

    Open the Grocery Compare toolbar icon and enable New World or Woolworths. Each store asks for its own access. Pin the icon from your browser’s Extensions menu for easy access.

  2. Connect the desktop app

    In Grocery Compare, open checkout → Set up browser companion → Connect browser. Use the same browser profile where you installed this extension.

  3. Open a store, sign in, then send

    Use the Cart buttons in the companion or Open browser in the app. Sign in on the retailer’s website. Return to the app and select Send items, then review the retailer cart before paying.

You’re in control

The companion only runs cart requests on your chosen grocery sites when the desktop app asks. It never runs on Google or email pages, reads your passwords, changes browser settings, or completes a payment.

Pause from the toolbar to stop new work. Turn off a store’s access whenever you like, or disconnect to remove this browser’s pairing. An already submitted cart request may still finish.

Help & updates

Updating from the old companion

Open chrome://extensions or edge://extensions, find Grocery Compare, and click Reload after updating the app. If you loaded a different folder, remove that old entry and use Load unpacked to select the new companion folder. Keep only one copy enabled. Enable your stores in the new popup, then reconnect from the app if needed.

Installing on another computer

Install Grocery Compare, open its companion folder from browser setup, then open your browser’s Extensions page. Enable Developer mode, choose Load unpacked, and select that folder. Keep the folder in place. This build is not yet listed in the extension stores.

Connected does not mean signed in

Ready means the companion can reach the desktop app. Store access means you granted permission. Neither checks your retailer login. Open the store and sign in there before sending items.

Google or email is having trouble

Disable the companion on the browser’s Extensions page and reload the affected website. This build has no access to those sites or their cookies. If the problem continues with the companion disabled, it needs separate diagnosis. Don’t clear cookies or reset your browser as a setup step.

The app cannot connect

Keep Grocery Compare open and use Connect browser in its setup dialog. If that opens another browser, copy the whole connection-page address into this browser immediately; the code expires after two minutes. If a connection expired, generate a fresh one from the app. Avoid sharing that address.
