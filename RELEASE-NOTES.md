Grocery Compare 0.2.0: house-brand comparisons and browsing.

- Automatically compare suitable Pams/Value and Woolworths/Essentials house-brand products while preserving size, type and explicit variant distinctions.
- Show both names and label house-brand comparisons as equivalents.
- Browse eight departments through shared aisles mapped to live retailer category trees.
- Filter loaded results by brand, house brands, specials and availability.
- Open Similar items to find and add alternatives from both stores.
- Retain category, dietary-tag, special and health-star metadata for future features.

Your saved shopping profile is preserved. Ingredient-level equality is not implied by a house-brand match.

Validated with 35 unit tests and hidden native Electron checks covering live category browsing, filters, house-brand pairs, similar-item additions, basket editing, persistence and the checkout dialog.

Authenticated cart transfer is experimental and has not been verified end to end. Each store requires its own login and may require manual verification. Final checkout and totals remain with the retailer. The installer is unsigned.
